Who We Are

Company
Profile

GPSL EXPRESS is a fast-growing Cambodian logistics provider specializing in efficient domestic and international shipping. Headquartered in Phnom Penh, we offer a complete, one-stop China-Cambodia supply chain solution built for local businesses that want to source, ship, and scale with confidence.

Product Sourcing — Direct procurement from China's top e-commerce platforms, workshops, and factories (OEM/ODM), so Cambodian businesses can buy at factory-direct pricing without navigating the process alone.

Flexible Shipping — Multimodal freight by land, sea, and air, handling everything from small packages to large commercial cargo, moved directly to your destination.

Our Mission

At GPSL EXPRESS, our mission is to build a trusted logistics network that serves as a vital bridge for bilateral trade.

We connect Cambodian entrepreneurs directly to China's supply chain with factory-direct pricing, and drive local growth by shipping high-quality Cambodian products to international markets efficiently, safely, and at the most competitive costs.
